For most of our lives we identify with our body, our name, our memories and our place in society. But which of those is our real self? And what remains once we leave the physical body behind?

The video turns our attention inward and reminds us that the answers to the fundamental questions of life cannot be found through the outer world or through intellectual knowledge alone. Real understanding comes through self-knowledge, meditation and direct inner experience.

Sensei Rajeev's discourse is an invitation to reflect more deeply on the nature of consciousness, of life and of death — and on where our path leads once we leave the body.
