We have set out from the Czech Republic to India once again. After landing in New Delhi, the journey to the Karauli Sarkar ashram awaited us — a place we don't return to as tourists, but as disciples wishing to deepen their spiritual practice here for several weeks.

Our journey began at the airport in New Delhi. From the bustle of the Indian capital we continued by minibus further inland, toward a place we already know well. We come to the Karauli Sarkar ashram now for the umpteenth time — and yet every return has its own unique atmosphere.

After the long journey, an unforgettable welcome awaited us at the gates of the ashram. The moment we stepped off the minibus, we were showered with flowers. In a single instant, the fatigue of travel turned into joy, gratitude, and the feeling of being back where we belong.

We will now spend several weeks at the ashram filled with meditation, spiritual practice, teaching and life together. The video captures not only our journey from New Delhi, but above all a return to a place that has become an important part of our spiritual path.
