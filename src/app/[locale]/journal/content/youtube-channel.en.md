## Discover the living wisdom of Bharat with us

We are glad to introduce the new Zero Balance Society YouTube channel. It was created as a space for sharing authentic spiritual practice, traditional knowledge and personal experience from India — Bharat.

On the channel you will gradually find guided meditations and sadhanas with Gurudev, havans, rituals, deeksha and footage from life in the ashram. It also includes video lessons with Sensei Rajeev Sinha and recordings from our journeys through the sacred places, the countryside and the everyday life of Bharat.

We publish videos in Czech, English and Hindi. For selected recordings we are preparing Czech dubbing or subtitles, so that the traditional teaching is open to as wide a circle as possible.

Visit the Zero Balance Society YouTube channel, subscribe, and share this journey with us.
